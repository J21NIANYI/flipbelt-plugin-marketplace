# Foundation source policy

Required envelope fields are `schema_version`, `release_id`, `source_commit`, source identifier, hash when supplied, and retrieval time/current session. A current published page or controlled asset is A-level evidence. Missing release metadata, unknown identifiers, inaccessible assets, or conflicting envelopes produce E/Unknown.

Assets may support only the claim they actually show. A temporary authorized asset response must not be converted into a permanent public URL.
