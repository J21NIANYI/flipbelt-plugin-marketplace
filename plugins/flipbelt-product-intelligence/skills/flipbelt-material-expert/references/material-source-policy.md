# Material source policy

Priority: published FlipBelt composition/structure/test claim; then applicable official standard or primary research; then reliable professional reference. Marketing copy, retailer descriptions, unsourced tables, and generic fiber folklore cannot establish a product claim.

Weather can describe exposure context only. Running Expert owns the running-task meaning of that context.

For detail-page review, retain every published qualifier on composition, structure, finish, test sample, color, method, conditions, garment-versus-material scope, and extrapolation boundary. A stronger target claim without matching current-release evidence is `NO-SOURCE`; a direct contradiction is `FAIL`; an omitted mandatory qualifier is `MISSING`.
