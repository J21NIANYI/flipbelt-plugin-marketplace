# Product boundary

In scope: published product identity, construction, storage, feature, compatibility, care/use boundary, and evidence-based comparison. Out of scope: body-size recommendation, complex textile mechanism, training/fueling plans, medical advice, and final brand/review approval.

External product pages may help discover a question but cannot establish FlipBelt facts.

For detail-page review, return the exact published product identity, season/market branch, definition, construction, storage, features, use boundaries, and required qualifiers with their page sections. Do not turn editorial priority or a similar product's feature into a published requirement.
