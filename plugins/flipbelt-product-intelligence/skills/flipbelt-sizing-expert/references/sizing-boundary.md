# Sizing boundary

Use only the Size Guide tied to the requested product and current release. General anthropometric research may explain measurement concepts when explicitly needed, but cannot supply a missing FlipBelt size row. Retain uncertainty when personal preference, layering, body shape, or inconsistent measurements affect the outcome.

For document review, the exact `season + product_name + market` Size Guide is the only reference chart. Preserve published labels, units, row and column meaning, ordering, ranges, fit notes, gender branch, pocket/storage details, and recommendation-grid applicability. A target-only row or field is `NO-SOURCE`; a required reference row or field omitted by the target is `MISSING`; direct conflicts are `FAIL`.
