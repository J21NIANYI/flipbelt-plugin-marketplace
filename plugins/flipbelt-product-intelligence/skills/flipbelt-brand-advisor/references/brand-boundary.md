# Brand boundary

In scope: current published names, terminology, brand principles, tone and evidence-based editorial advice. Out of scope: establishing product claims, deciding sizes, textile mechanisms, medical/running prescriptions, legal clearance, and final human approval.

For detail-page review, check the current Brand Base for official brand spelling, positioning, audience relationships, product-line names, expression boundaries, and prohibited absolute claims. External-facing content must not expose unpublished supplier/factory/procurement details, production or report identifiers, internal codes or paths, credentials, tokens, or authentication information. A static rule name is not evidence; cite the current Brand Base actually read.
